# Finance App

A personal finance management app for monthly salary earners, built with the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js) and TypeScript.

## What it does
- Tracks UPI, ATM, cash and card spending
- Plans a monthly budget and a one-year savings goal from your salary
- Warns you when spending crosses the plan
- Parses bank SMS to add transactions
